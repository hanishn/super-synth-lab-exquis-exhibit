# Songs rights and provenance

## Scope

The Songs family contains original, single-line Exquis exercise arrangements
made from the public-domain melodies listed below. It ships neither lyrics,
sound recordings, artwork, commercial lead sheets, nor any third-party modern
arrangement.

This is a U.S.-focused provenance record, not legal advice. Before a commercial
release in a particular territory, obtain qualified review for that territory,
including title/trademark and any content added after this record.

## Included source melodies

| Song | Source status used by this project | What is shipped |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Frere Jacques | Traditional French melody; Library of Congress catalog documents it in a collection designated public domain/free to reuse. | Original C-major Exquis melody study. |
| Ode to Joy | Beethoven's Symphony No. 9 (1824); IMSLP identifies the score as public domain. | Original C-major melody study, no Schiller text. |
| Auld Lang Syne | Traditional Scottish tune; Library of Congress public-domain/free-to-reuse collections include nineteenth-century editions. | Original C-major melody study, no Burns text. |
| Oh! Susanna | Stephen Foster, 1848; historical Library of Congress material is designated public domain/free to reuse. | Original C-major melody study, no lyrics. |
| Simple Gifts | Joseph Brackett, 1848; historical Shaker melody. | Original C-major melody study, no lyrics or later arrangements. |
| Greensleeves | Traditional English melody dating to the sixteenth century. | Original A-minor Exquis melody study. |

## Maintenance rule

Do not add a song merely because it is familiar. Add it only after recording:

1. composer/traditional provenance and publication/death-date evidence;
2. the jurisdictions covered by the conclusion;
3. that the implementation uses a project-authored study, not a copied modern
   arrangement, recording, lyric, or branded presentation.
