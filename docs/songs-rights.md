# Songs rights and provenance

## Scope

The Songs family contains original, single-line Exquis exercise arrangements
made from the public-domain melodies listed below. It ships neither lyrics,
sound recordings, artwork, commercial lead sheets, nor any third-party modern
arrangement.

This is a U.S.-focused provenance record, not legal advice. Before a commercial
release in a particular territory, obtain qualified review for that territory,
including title/trademark and any content added after this record.

## Maintenance rule

Do not add a song merely because it is familiar. Add it only after recording:

1. composer/traditional provenance and publication/death-date evidence;
2. the jurisdictions covered by the conclusion;
3. that the implementation uses a project-authored study, not a copied modern
   arrangement, recording, lyric, or branded presentation.
